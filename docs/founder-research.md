# Founder research

This note records public sources consulted for the About-page founder highlight. The UI uses only concise claims supported by these sources.

## Verified facts used in the interface

- The founder is identified as **Tarzaa Anzenge Gerald Caesar**, professionally **Cza of REM**.
- Public profiles describe him as a Nigerian entertainment visionary, talent manager, industry strategist, and founder/CEO associated with Royal Empire Management.
- Public biography material associates him with RADARCharts, MOTHERLand, XCAPE, and the REM LIVE AID Foundation.
- A public LinkedIn profile places Royal Empire Management in Abuja, Nigeria and lists founder/CEO roles.
- The founder's stated philosophy includes protecting music and fans.
