# Design Brief – Enterprise Creative Agency & Production House

## Brand character
- Cinematic, confident, minimal.
- Feels like a high-end production studio, not a generic "digital agency template".
- Visual language should suggest film, craft, and precision.

## Non-negotiables
- No generic AI aesthetics, no cookie-cutter Bootstrapy layouts.
- No chaotic, random animations.
- No tiny, low-contrast text or cluttered sections.

## Visual direction

### Typography
- Strong typographic scale with clear hierarchy.
- Large, confident headings; comfortable body text.

### Layout
- Generous whitespace.
- Clear grid, but not rigidly symmetrical everywhere.
- Occasional "hero moments" that break the grid in a controlled way.

### Color
- Mostly neutral foundation (black / off-black, white / off-white, greys).
- One or two accent colors used sparingly for focus and interaction states.

## Motion direction
- Slow, confident, cinematic pacing.
- Consistent easing (no random bounces or elastic overshoot).
- Scroll-driven storytelling on key pages (landing, case studies, process).
- Micro-interactions that feel precise, not flashy.

## Pages to design (for now)
Focus on these first:
1. Landing page (`/`)
2. One flagship case study (`/work/[slug]`)
3. Work grid (`/work`)
4. Client dashboard (`/dashboard`) – high-level concept

Use these as the foundation for the rest of the site.
